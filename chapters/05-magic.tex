% REAL (from Notion): "Magic" is whatever makes a setting feel fantastic.
\lettrine{M}{agic} is whatever makes a setting feel fantastic. It is
literal magic in a high-fantasy setting, hacking in a cyberpunk world,
science and engineering in Star Trek, the Force in Star Wars. Magic lets
you do the impossible, the improbable, and the fantastic. When you enter
a realm, decide together what Magic \emph{looks like} there.

\section{What Magic Covers}
Magic wears a different face in every realm. Depending on the setting, it
might include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Spell casting
  \item True-name magic
  \item Computer hacking
  \item Demon summoning
  \item Astral projection
  \item Using the Force
  \item Tinkering
  \item Engineering, in a high-science setting
\end{itemize}

\section{Magic Tags}
When you create your character, choose \keyword{magic tags} --- as many as
your Magic rank allows (\dice{3} at rank~2, \dice{7} at rank~3, \dice{12}
at rank~4). Every act of magic pairs what you \emph{do} (a Technique) with
what you \emph{affect} (a Form): \emph{create}~+~\emph{fire} to kindle a
flame, \emph{perceive}~+~\emph{mind} to read a thought. The Techniques and
Forms are drawn from \emph{Ars Magica}; the tag-selection system is
inspired by John Harper's \emph{Lady Blackbird}. A tag's meaning bends to
fit the realm --- \emph{control} compels a beast in a fantasy world and a
security system in the sprawl.

\subsection{Techniques --- what you do}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \keyword{Create} --- Produce something from nothing, or restore a thing to its whole, ideal state.
  \item \keyword{Perceive} --- Sense, read, and learn; uncover the hidden connections between things.
  \item \keyword{Transform} --- Change one thing into another, and direct the mechanisms of change itself.
  \item \keyword{Destroy} --- Break down, wither, and unmake; hasten damage, decay, and dissolution.
  \item \keyword{Control} --- Move and command matter, or compel the actions of living things.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Forms --- what you affect}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \keyword{Animal} --- Beasts of every kind, from the fish of the sea to the birds of the air.
  \item \keyword{Water} --- Liquids of all kinds, from a roaring flood to a still, clear pool.
  \item \keyword{Air} --- Air, wind, and weather; the medium of true flight.
  \item \keyword{Body} --- Humans and humanlike bodies, and those that once had souls.
  \item \keyword{Plant} --- Plants and trees, and all plant matter --- even dead wood and cloth.
  \item \keyword{Fire} --- Fire, heat, and light in all their restless forms.
  \item \keyword{Image} --- Illusions and phantasms; the appearance of things, never their substance.
  \item \keyword{Mind} --- Minds, thoughts, memories, and emotions --- and incorporeal spirits.
  \item \keyword{Earth} --- Solids, especially earth and stone: the foundation of the world.
  \item \keyword{Magic} --- Raw magical power itself; the stuff of spells and the beings made from it.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Specific Tags --- your own}
You may also choose \emph{any} tag you like, so long as it is more specific
in application than the Techniques and Forms above. A specific tag is
narrower, but it stacks with them for a bigger bonus. A few examples:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \keyword{Gems} --- Precious stones and crystals, cut or raw.
  \item \keyword{Smoke} --- Smoke, mist, and haze.
  \item \keyword{Thorns} --- Barbs, brambles, and things that wound.
  \item \keyword{Hearing} --- Sound and the sense of it.
  \item \keyword{Reading} --- Written words and their meaning.
  \item \keyword{Protection} --- Wards, shields, and defence.
  \item \keyword{Throwing} --- Hurling and propelling objects.
  \item \keyword{Flying} --- Flight and being borne aloft.
\end{itemize}

\section{How Magic Works}
Whenever you do magic, you may add \keyword{one unit to your roll for each
applicable tag you have}, up to your Magic level (\dice{1}--\dice{4}). The
GM sets the difficulty.

\begin{example}
Rooster is a level~3 magic user with the tags \emph{create, destroy, fire,
water, food, flying}. They want to conjure a can of soda. They may apply
\emph{create}, \emph{food}, and \emph{water} --- getting \dice{+3}.
\end{example}
