% REAL (from Notion): "Magic" is whatever makes a setting feel fantastic.
\lettrine{M}{agic} is whatever makes a setting feel fantastic. It is
literal magic in a high-fantasy setting, hacking in a cyberpunk world,
science and engineering in Star Trek, the Force in Star Wars. Magic lets
you do the impossible, the improbable, and the fantastic. When you enter
a realm, decide together what Magic \emph{looks like} there.

\section{What Magic Covers}
Magic wears a different face in every realm. Depending on the setting, it
might include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Spell casting
  \item True-name magic
  \item Computer hacking
  \item Demon summoning
  \item Astral projection
  \item Using the Force
  \item Tinkering
  \item Engineering, in a high-science setting
\end{itemize}

\section{Magic Tags}
When you create your character, choose \keyword{magic tags} from the lists
below --- as many as your Magic rank allows (\dice{3} at rank~2, \dice{7}
at rank~3, \dice{12} at rank~4). This tag system is inspired by John
Harper's \emph{Lady Blackbird}.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=6.5em, style=nextline, font=\normalfont\itshape]
  \item[Techniques] Create, perceive, transform, destroy, control.
  \item[Forms] Animal, water, air, body, plant, fire, image, mind, earth, magic.
\end{description}

\noindent In addition, you may choose \emph{any} tag you like, as long as
it is more specific in application than the tags above. For example:
\begin{quote}\itshape
Gems, smoke, thorns, hearing, reading, protection, throwing, flying.
\end{quote}

\section{How Magic Works}
Whenever you do magic, you may add \keyword{one unit to your roll for each
applicable tag you have}, up to your Magic level (\dice{1}--\dice{4}). The
GM sets the difficulty.

\begin{example}
Rooster is a level~3 magic user with the tags \emph{create, destroy, fire,
water, food, flying}. They want to conjure a can of soda. They may apply
\emph{create}, \emph{food}, and \emph{water} --- getting \dice{+3}.
\end{example}
