\lipsum[21][1-3]

\lettrine{S}{ome things} you simply \emph{do}. A roll asks whether you
manage something; a \keyword{Special Feat} means the question never
comes up. You fly. You breathe in the void. You know when you are being
lied to. No dice, no effort, no doubt.

\begin{statblock}[Special Feats Granted, Any Column]

\rating{2} & None.\\[3pt]
\rating{3} & \dice{1} Special Feat.\\[3pt]
\rating{4} & \dice{2} Special Feats in total.\\
\end{tabularx}
\end{statblock}

Each column grants Feats separately. A character at rank \rating{3} in
both Body and Magic has two Feats: one Body Feat and one Magic Feat.

% ---- Section 1: Rules ------------------------------------------------
\section{How Feats Work}

\subsection*{Always On}
A Feat never needs a roll. It has no uses to count and nothing to
recharge. If it describes something you are, it is always true. If it
describes something you do, you do it whenever you like.

\subsection*{The Fiction, Not the Dice}
A Feat never adds dice, removes dice, or changes a target number. It
changes what is \emph{true}, and the GM calls for rolls based on what is
true. If you are invisible, the guards' eyes are no obstacle, and there
is no roll to slip past them --- unless their dogs can still smell you,
in which case \emph{that} is the roll.

\subsection*{Hard Things Are Still Rolls}
Doing something difficult \emph{with} a Feat is an ordinary roll.
Flying is free; outflying a dragon is a \keyword{Body} roll. Hearing a
guard's thoughts is free; prying loose the secret she is trying not to
think about is a spell.

\subsection*{Limits Are the Point}
A Feat does exactly what it says and no more. Its limits are part of
the Feat, and they are what keep it fair. When a situation is unclear,
the GM reads the Feat narrowly.

\section{Choosing Your Feats}

When a column reaches rank \rating{3} or \rating{4}, choose a Feat from
that column's list below. Each column's Feats are pitched differently on
purpose:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \keyword{Magic} Feats are the strongest and most versatile.
    Reality bends around you.
  \item \keyword{Body} Feats are broadly strong and useful. They are
    what you \emph{are}, and no one can take them from you.
  \item \keyword{Stuff} Feats are great, but situational. Each is a thing
    you own, and it can be lost or taken like any other Stuff. It returns
    when you cross into a new realm (see \emph{Losing Your Stuff} in
    \emph{Stuff}). A Stuff Feat costs no Stuff Points and has no Tier.
  \item \keyword{Skills} Feats are mundane and a little weaker. Nothing
    on the list is impossible, only legendary.
\end{itemize}
You may also build your own Feat with your GM. Measure it against its
column's list: it should feel about as strong as its neighbours.

\subsection*{Feats Across the Realms}
Your Feats travel with you and re-skin to fit each new realm, like your
tags. Flight might be leathery wings in one world and hip-mounted
thrusters in the next. Stuff Feats re-kit along with the rest of your
gear.

\renewcommand{\realmaccent}{columnMagic}
\section{Magic Feats}
\artinline{feat-magic-stormcaller.png}

\begin{feat}{Blink}
Teleport to anywhere you can see within a stone's throw, as often as you
like. You take only yourself and what you carry.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Far-Caster}
Your spells reach anything you can see, however far away: the far side
of a valley, a ship on the horizon, a face in a tower window.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Hearth}
Wherever you rest for the night, spirits, scrying, and hostile magic
cannot reach you or the companions sleeping near you. Swords still can.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Home-Anchor}
Choose one place. From anywhere in the same realm, an hour of stillness
brings you back to it. To move your anchor, sleep somewhere new for a
week. It resets when you cross into a new realm.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Lasting Works}
Your spells last until you end them or someone breaks them. A wall of
ice stays standing, a charm holds, a light keeps burning.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Legion}
Any spell you can cast on one person, you can cast on everyone you can
see, with the same roll. You choose who is caught and who is spared.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Mind-Hearing}
You always hear the surface thoughts of the people near you: whatever
they are thinking right now. You cannot shut it off, and crowds are
loud. Deeper secrets still take a spell.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Phase}
Walk through walls, doors, and floors as if they were mist. You cannot
stop inside solid matter or bring anyone with you, and magical wards
still hold you back.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Realm-Sense}
You know when something does not belong in the realm you are in: other
travellers, smuggled artifacts, and the thin places where realms meet.
You can feel which way they lie.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Siege-Mage}
Your spells work at the scale of buildings. What would break a door
breaks a gatehouse; what would knock down a soldier topples a tower.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Spirit-Sight}
You always see ghosts, spirits, invisible creatures, and magic itself:
auras, wards, curses, and spells as they are woven.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Stormcaller}
The weather within sight follows your will. Clearing the sky or calling
a storm takes a few minutes, not a roll. Putting lightning exactly where
you want it is still a spell.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Tongue of All Things}
Speak with animals, plants, spirits, and objects, and understand their
answers. They know only what they know: a door remembers who passed
through it, not their names.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Unseen}
Become invisible at will, along with everything you carry. Sound,
scent, and footprints still give you away.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Wards}
Hostile spells cast on anyone or anything near you simply fail. Only a
spell aimed at you directly gets through.
\end{feat}

\renewcommand{\realmaccent}{columnBody}
\section{Body Feats}
\artinline{feat-body-shapeshifter.png}

\begin{feat}{Breathe Anywhere}
You do not need air. Water, smoke, poison gas, and the vacuum of space
cannot suffocate you. Cold, heat, and pressure are another matter.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Burrower}
Dig through earth, sand, and snow as fast as you walk, leaving a tunnel
behind you or letting it collapse. Solid rock and worked stone stop you.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Dark-Sight}
You see in total darkness as well as in dim daylight, though colours
wash out to grey. Magical darkness still blinds you.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Flight}
Fly under your own power, as fast as you can run, for as long as you
like. Carrying another person slows you to a walk.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Hardy}
Extreme heat, cold, pressure, and radiation do you no harm: desert noon,
arctic night, the deep sea floor. Fire and lava still burn.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Iron Gut}
You can eat anything organic and be nourished by it. Poison and disease
pass through you harmlessly.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Keen Nose}
Track by scent like a bloodhound, even on a trail hours old. You can
smell fear, sickness, and poison in a cup. Rain and strong smells cover
a trail.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Mimic}
After a few minutes of studying someone, you can copy their face,
voice, and build. You do not get their clothes, their fingerprints, or
their memories.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Regenerator}
Injuries that would take others weeks to heal are gone by morning. Lost
limbs grow back within a week, and nothing leaves a scar. You still go
down when you are hurt; you just come back whole.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Shapeshifter}
You have one other form, chosen when you take this Feat: a wolf, a hawk,
a great bear. Changing takes a moment, and your gear does not change
with you.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Sixth Sense}
You cannot be ambushed. You feel danger a heartbeat before it arrives,
so you are never caught asleep or struck from behind unawares. Knowing
it is coming is not the same as dodging it.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Size-Shift}
Shrink to the size of a mouse, grow to twice your height, or stop
anywhere in between. Your Stats do not change; what you can reach and
fit through does.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Squeeze}
You fit through any gap your head fits through: a keyhole-sized vent, a
gap under a door, a drainpipe. It takes time, so not in the middle of a
fight.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Tireless}
You never need to sleep and never tire from exertion. You can march all
day and keep watch all night, every night.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Wall-Crawler}
Walk on walls and ceilings as easily as floors, with your hands free.
Ice, oil, and glass defeat you.
\end{feat}

\renewcommand{\realmaccent}{columnStuff}
\section{Stuff Feats}
\artinline{feat-stuff-pocket-refuge.png}

\begin{feat}{Bottomless Bag}
Reach in and pull out any ordinary item that could fit in the bag: rope,
a lantern, a crowbar, a change of clothes. Nothing valuable, nothing
magical, nothing you couldn't buy at a market.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Cloaking Suit}
While you wear it and move slowly, you are invisible. Running or
fighting makes you flicker back into view.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Comm-Link}
A set of links for you and your party. Anyone wearing one can talk to
the others at any distance within the realm. Anyone who takes one can
listen in.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Companion}
A loyal companion with a mind of its own: a hound, a squire, a droid. It
follows you and helps where it can, and it can be hurt, lost, or
captured.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Crew}
A handful of people who follow your orders: sailors, thugs, technicians.
They are not heroes, and they break when things go badly.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Flight Rig}
A jetpack, grav-boots, or a winged harness. While you wear it, you fly.
It is loud and obvious.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Informant Network}
In any city, after a day, you have eyes and ears reporting to you: who
has arrived, who is asking about you, what is for sale. Outside cities,
it goes quiet.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Letters of Marque}
Papers granting you legal authority. Officials treat you as having every
right to be where you are. Outlaws and rival powers don't care.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Master Key}
Opens any ordinary lock. Warded, guarded, or truly clever locks are
another matter.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Patron}
A powerful backer who takes your call: a duke, a corporation, a temple.
They help when it suits them, and they will ask favours in return.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Pocket Refuge}
A door (chalked on a wall, a folding tent, a hatch) that opens into a
small, safe room only you and your guests can enter. Whoever holds the
door holds the room.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Rebreather}
While you wear it, you can breathe anywhere: underwater, in smoke, in
vacuum. Masks can be torn off and suits can be punctured.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Scanner}
Point it at something and see through walls, crates, and skin on its
display. It takes a moment, and anyone watching can see what you are
doing.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Translator}
While it is on you, you understand and are understood in any spoken
language, human or not. There is a lag, and subtlety gets lost.
\end{feat}

\renewcommand{\realmaccent}{columnSkills}
\section{Skills Feats}
\artinline{feat-skills-quick-change.png}

\begin{feat}{Always Armed}
Searched, stripped, or jailed, you still have one small weapon: a
blade, a garrote, something you can improvise.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Appraiser}
At a glance, you know what an object is worth, roughly where and when it
was made, and whether it is a fake. Magic items tell you only that they
are magic.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Architect's Eye}
Walk around a building, or through one floor of it, and you know its
layout, where the walls are thin, and where someone would put a vault.
Not what is inside.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Beast-Friend}
Animals never attack you unless they are provoked, starving, or
commanded. That doesn't mean they do what you say.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Coroner}
Examine a body and you know how the person died, roughly when, and
whether they were moved. Not who did it.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Fixer}
In any town, within a day, you find out who sells what, legal or not.
They still set the price.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Forgettable}
No one remembers your face afterwards unless you gave them a reason to.
Cameras and portraits still work.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Honest Face}
Strangers assume you belong wherever you are: a servant, a guest, a
local. It lasts until you do something that doesn't belong.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Lie-Sense}
You can tell when someone lies to your face, if you can see them and
speak their language. You learn that it is a lie, not what the truth
is, and half-truths slip past.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Never Lost}
You always know where you are, which way is north, and the way back to
anywhere you have been. In a new realm, this takes a day to settle in.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Polyglot}
You become fluent in any human language after a day of hearing it. Your
accent always gives you away.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Quick-Change}
In a minute, with whatever is to hand, you disguise yourself well enough
to pass at a glance as a different kind of person: a guard, a servant, a
priest. Not as a specific person.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Read the Room}
When you walk into a scene, you know who is in charge, who is armed, and
who is nervous.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Survivalist}
In the wild, you and a few companions never go hungry, thirsty, or
without shelter. Where there is truly nothing, you cannot find it.
\end{feat}

\begin{feat}{Unforgettable}
Anyone who meets you remembers you and your name forever, for good or
ill.
\end{feat}

\section{World-Granted Feats}

Some realms grant Feats to everyone who enters them. Each realm's
chapter says which Feats you get.
