\lettrine{B}{ody} is your physical self --- the shape and substance you
carry from world to world. It sets your three core \keyword{Stats} and
determines what enhancements you can take during character creation.

% ---- Section 1: Character Creation ---------------------------------
\section{At Character Creation}

% REAL RULE: Body rank maps directly to starting Stat allocation.
\begin{statblock}[Body Ranks]
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.16\linewidth} Y@{}}
\rating{1} & Ordinary person; base stats across the board.\\[3pt]
\rating{2} & Stand out in some way --- strong, quick, or striking.\\[3pt]
\rating{3} & Exceptional --- elven grace, ogrish might, uncanny beauty.\\[3pt]
\rating{4} & Truly strange or monstrous --- a dragon, an angel.\\
\end{tabularx}
\end{statblock}

Assign one of the four priorities (A through D) to Body. Your rank
tells you how to set your core Stats and which \keyword{Modifications}
you may take.

\textbf{Body \rating{1} --- Ordinary Person.}
Each of your three Stats begins at \dice{1}. Choose two Stats and add
\dice{+1} to each.

\begin{example}
Mara is an athletic but unremarkable wanderer. Body \rating{1}. All three
Stats start at 1. She boosts Body and Mind: \textbf{Body~2, Mind~2,
Face~1}.
\end{example}

\textbf{Body \rating{2} --- Someone Who Stands Out.}
Each of your three Stats begins at \dice{1}. You may then take up to two
\keyword{Modifications} from the list in Your Modifications below.

\begin{example}
Kira is a charming fixer. Body \rating{2}. Stats start at 1. She takes
\emph{Striking} and \emph{Sharp-Minded}: \textbf{Body~1, Mind~3, Face~3}.
\end{example}

% ---- Section 2: Rules -----------------------------------------------
\section{Body in Play}

Your Stats are the base of every roll. Add the relevant Stat to the relevant
Skill --- or to the Magic tags that apply --- and roll that many \dice{d6}s;
each die showing \dice{4}, \dice{5}, or \dice{6} is a success.

The \keyword{Body} stat serves double duty: it measures raw physical power
\emph{and} how much harm you can shrug off before you go down.
Body rank also determines what physical form you take when you cross
into a new realm --- an exceptional body stays exceptional, but it wears
a different shape. See each genre chapter for local translations.
