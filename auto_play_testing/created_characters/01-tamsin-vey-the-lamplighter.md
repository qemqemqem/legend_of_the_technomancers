# Tamsin Vey, the Lamplighter

*Built by: Claude Opus 5.5 (main session), 2026-09-23. Rules source: `rulebook.tex` + `chapters/`.*

## Concept

A lighthouse keeper on a cliff where the sea ate ships. One winter she drowned
trying to reach a wreck, and the sea gave her back. When the wound in the
world opened, her lighthouse came with her. Now it washes up on the shore of
every realm she lands in: a stone tower in fantasy, a rusting beacon-spire
over the flooded sprawl, a nav-buoy station in deep space. She can't bring
herself to leave it dark. She's watchful and weather-wise, and she's bad
with people.

## Stats

4 Stat Points from Body rank 2, 0 from Skills rank 1.

- **Body 2** (1 pt) · **Mind 3** (2 pts) · **Face 2** (1 pt)
- **Weird 2** (Magic 3 − 1)
- **Pools:** Body 2 / Mind 3 / Face 2

## Body Tag (1)

- `Keen Senses`: a keeper's eyes, used to reading the night water.

## Skills (4)

- `Perception`, `Piloting`, `Craft`, `Lore`

## Magic (7 tags, 1 Feat): storm-witchery

- **Techniques:** `Perceive`, `Control`
- **Forms:** `Fire`, `Air`, `Water`
- **Specific:** `Light` (narrower than Fire), `Storms` (narrower than Air/Water)
- **Feat: Needs No Air.** The sea gave her back. She can't drown, and she can't suffocate.

## Stuff (12 points, 7 tags, 2 Feats, Lifestyle: Rich)

| Item | Tier | Cost | Tags |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Lighthouse** (Fortress) | **4 → 5 capstone** | 4 | `Watchful`, `Fortified` |
| **The Gannet**, storm-cutter (Vehicle) | 4 | 4 | `Rugged`, `Agile` |
| **Stormglass Lantern** (Gadget: its beam cuts fog and signals for miles) | 2 | 2 | `Fog-Piercing` |

- **Feat: See Through Solid Matter.** The Keeper's Loupe, a shard cut from the great lens.
- **Feat: Never Sleep.** The Keeper's Watch. She winds it instead of sleeping.

## Four Scenarios

### 1. High Fantasy: longboats in the dark

Pirates are rowing toward the harbour with their lanterns shuttered. Tamsin
climbs to the lamp room and sweeps the great beam across the water, pouring
her sight into the light to count the boats and find them.

- **Base:** Mind, acting *through* the Lighthouse → Max(Tier 5, Mind 3) = **5**
- **Tags:** `Keen Senses` + `Perception` + `Watchful` + `Perceive` + `Light` = **5 (at the cap)**
- **Pool: 10 dice.** This is the biggest pool the game allows. `Fog-Piercing`
  would make a sixth tag, but it's on the lantern, not the tower, and the
  cap blocks it anyway.

### 2. Cyberpunk: canal chase

Corporate drones chase the Gannet (now a scarred hydrofoil) through the
flooded sprawl. A flood-gate full of debris is grinding shut, and she guns
it straight through the gap.

- **Base:** Body, acting through the Gannet → Max(Tier 4, Body 2) = **4**
- **Tags:** `Piloting` + `Agile` (threading the gap) + `Rugged` (smashing through debris) = **3**
- **Pool: 7 dice**

### 3. Space Adventure: the harbourmaster says no

A station harbourmaster won't let the party dock. Tamsin has no social
skills, so she falls back on the oldest law she knows: the Law of Safe
Harbour, which no keeper or harbourmaster may refuse a ship in distress.
She shames him with it.

- **Base:** Face **2** (his will resists)
- **Tags:** `Lore`, if the GM agrees it clearly applies. Skills list a
  default Stat, but "other Stats may apply if the fiction supports it." = **1**
- **Pool: 3 dice.** This is her weak spot. With only 2 points in her Face
  pool, two bad exchanges leave her at −1 die on every Face roll.

### 4. High Fantasy: the wreck

The ship goes down. Tamsin walks the sea floor with **no roll needed**
(Needs No Air), then finds a sailor pinned under a fallen mast. She commands
the sea itself to lift the timber.

- **Base:** Body **2**. The mast's weight resists, and magic doesn't change
  that. This is the rulebook's "should my wizard get buff?" case.
- **Tags:** `Control` + `Water` = **2**
- **Pool: 4 dice.** Her magic is strong but her arms are weak, so she's only
  middling at heavy lifting.

## Things Noticed While Building

- **The Tier-5 capstone is very strong on its home ground.** A Stuff-A
  character with a Tier-5 Location plus a few tags that fit reaches the
  10-die ceiling easily. The trade-off is that it only works when she's
  acting *through* the tower, which probably balances it. Worth playtesting.
- **Stuff-granted Feats and paying for items:** the rules say a Stuff Feat
  "usually comes from one specific, ownable item," but they don't say
  whether that item costs Stuff Points. Treated the Loupe and the Watch as
  free props that come with the Feats. The rules should spell this out.
- **Skills D is harsh for Face:** a character with no social skills and
  Face 2 really does roll only 2–3 dice. Likely intended.

## Reflection on the Build Process

**What seemed good**
- ABCD priorities make the build fast and force a real trade-off. Deciding
  "Stuff A, Skills D" told me who Tamsin was before I'd picked anything else.
- One formula for everything (Stat + tags, count 4+) meant every scenario
  resolved in seconds once the Stat was chosen.
- "What resists?" was right in 3 of 4 scenarios without argument.

**What seemed bad**
- Tier 5 plus a Location tag plus three other tags reaches 10 dice on her
  home ground with no effort. The 10-die ceiling became her *normal* roll.
- Stuff tiers only matter where a Stat is weak. Her Mind 3 is overridden by
  the tower, but a Mind-5 keeper would have gained nothing from it.

**What was cool**
- Re-Kitting on Arrival. A lighthouse that washes up on every shore is a
  campaign hook in itself, and the rules support it with no extra machinery.
- Stat and pool being the same number makes her lopsided spread (Face 2)
  hurt in a way that shows up in the story.
- Getting Needs No Air from "the sea gave her back" is a satisfying way to
  justify a Magic Feat.

**What was disappointing**
- The target-number guidance is still placeholder text, so I can't tell
  whether 4 dice is heroic or hopeless.
- Having 7 Magic tags rarely mattered. The cap and "does it clearly apply"
  meant 2–3 fired on any given spell.

**What was unclear**
- Whether a Stuff-Feat's item (the Loupe, the Watch) costs Stuff Points.
- Where "acting through" a Location stops. Does the whole island count as
  the lighthouse? Only the lamp room?
- Whether a Skill can pair with an off-default Stat (Lore → Face) without
  the GM's say-so.

**Recommendations to improve the system**
1. Write the target-number ladder before any further balance tuning;
   nearly every pool-size judgement depends on it.
2. Define the scope of "acting through" an item, especially Locations
   and Tier-5 capstones.
3. State outright whether Feat-granting items cost Stuff Points.
